# Project Overview

## Vision
A modern web application for intermediate to advanced trainees to plan, track, and optimize their workout routines, based on the principles of mesocycle training and advanced resistance training techniques.

## Motivation
Intermediate and advanced trainees often struggle to structure their training for long-term progress. Available apps frequently lack the ability to create periodized plans, support advanced workout methods, and offer clear, actionable progress analytics. Our solution aims to give dedicated users a comprehensive tool to plan and monitor their workouts, making evidence-based strength training accessible, structured, and motivating.

## Goals
- Allow users to sign up and create a personal profile.
- Provide a personal dashboard with progress graphs and a history of recent workouts, with weekly/monthly filtering options and access to past workouts.
- Enable users to build custom workout programs structured by mesocycles, including exercises paired with advanced techniques (such as myo reps, dropset, superset, cluster set, pre-exhaustion).
- Let users log each set and repetition for every exercise in a workout.
- Display muscle group–specific progress graphs (X-axis: time; Y-axis: weekly sets volume).
- Lay the foundation for additional features in the future (e.g., analytics, recommendations).
